League Rules

Keepers

The league's keeper rules are based on a 4 tier layout…

The league's keeper rules are based on a 4 tier layout. A GM may keep players from their final roster of the previous year in the 4 designated keeper rounds.

Keepers

Keeper Rounds

Keeper Rounds: 1, 4, 9, & 16

Keepers

Eligibility

Eligibility:

Keepers

Keeper Round 1

Keeper Round 1: Players drafted in rounds 1-3 in the previous season will be eligible to be kept in the 1st Round of the following season

Keepers

Keeper Round 4

Keeper Round 4: Players drafted in rounds 4-8 in the previous season will be eligible to be kept in the 4th Round of the following season

Keepers

Keeper Round 9

Keeper Round 9: Players drafted in or after the 9th round in the previous season will be eligible to be kept in the 9th Round of the following season

Keepers

Keeper Round 16

Keeper Round 16: Players that were undrafted and picked up through waivers will be eligible to be kept in the 16th round of the following season

Keepers

Keeping players for multiple years

Keeping players for multiple years: a player can be kept for a maximum of 4 years; if a GM elects to keep a player that was kept in the preceding year, that keepers eligibility will move down to the next tier Example: If a player was kept in the 9th round last year, the eligibility of that keeper will be moved down a tier and can be kept in the 4th round of the current draft year If a player was kept in the 1st Round of the previous years draft, that player will lose their keeper eligibility and will automatically be entered into the pool of free agents to be drafted

Keepers

Maximum # of Keepers

Maximum # of Keepers: A GM can keep as many players as they want, as long as they are kept in the eligible keeper rounds. Therefore, a GM can trade draft picks and obtain additional picks in the keeper rounds enabling them to keep more than one player in the same round

Keepers • Effective 2021

2020 AMENDMENT

2020 AMENDMENT: Effective 2021, The Maximum number of players a GM can keep is 4. This does not limit the GM to using 4 Different keeper slots (Example: Keeping a player in the first round, 1 player in the fourth round, and two players in the 16th Round)

Keepers

Keeper Due Dates & Extensions

Keeper Due Dates & Extensions: The Keeper due date will be announced at the same time the draft date will be announced. Typically, the keeper deadline will be one week before the draft date to ensure each GM has the time to prepare for the draft. If the keeper deadline is missed, a GM can request a 24 Hour Extension; however, a $10 fine will be imposed (taken from the GM's "DFU Deposit")

Each team will receive $100 budget for the regular season for blind bidding on the waiver wire.

Teams are able to add "waiver funds" as part of a trade package.

Waivers

Once draft results are submitted, all I drafted players…

Once draft results are submitted, all I drafted players will be locked and a waiver date will be set. Each team will receive $100 in pre-season funds. Pre-Season Waiver Budgets will reset to $0.00 once the initial waiver date passes and then each time will be set back to their initial $100 regular season budget (If you don't use it you lose it)

Don't Fuck Up Deposit

Each year, General Managers of the league will pay $10…

Each year, General Managers of the league will pay $10.00 in addition to their initial "buy-in" for that year to be used as a "Don't Fuck Up" deposit. This $10.00 will be held until the end of the regular season and returned back to the GM if said GM does not incur any instances of "Fucking Up" If a GM "Fucks Up", their deposit will not be returned and will be transferred to the league's Misc. Cash.

Don't Fuck Up Deposit

Fucking up is subjective and at the discretion of the league requiring a majority vote or 6/11 votes. (Note: the GM that fucked up will not participate in the vote)

Don't Fuck Up Deposit • Effective 2021

Events should only be considered fuck-ups if there is a…

Events should only be considered fuck-ups if there is a negative impact on one or more member(s) of the league "Fucked Up" Examples The following events are considered events of “Fucking up” -Starting an injured player -Starting a player on a bye -Not starting the best possible player for a particular position when there is a clear better option Example: Leaving a top rated player on your bench the week after a bye, leaving an RB3/4 in your lineup -Missing the Keeper Deadline and Requesting a 24 Hour Extension (2019 Amendment: The League vs. Christopher Polizois & Daniel Gutman) Fuckups incrementally increase by $5 per fuck up (Effective 2021)

Divisions & Schedule

Starting with the year 2020; Divisions will be changed…

Starting with the year 2020; Divisions will be changed for 3 to 2. The divisions will be re-aligned every 3 years and the two GM's with the best overall record, most points, best average finish over the course of those 3 years will Draft the other GM's into their respective Division in a Snake Draft Format. (1st Place gets 1st Pick, 2nd place gets 2nd & 3rd Pick, etc.)

Divisions & Schedule

Schedule

Schedule: Each team will play each other twice and the 2 teams form their division once. Over the course of 3 years you will have played Every team in the opposing conference 3 times, 1 team in your conference 7 times, Everyone else in your conference 6 times
